
# Household Energy Consumption and Weather Analysis

## Project Overview

An Mini project using Power BI to analyze household energy consumption and weather patterns through data analysis and visualizations.

## Objectives

- Analyze household energy consumption patterns.
- Explore relationships between electrical parameters and weather conditions.
- Identify hourly, monthly, and seasonal trends.
- Examine temperature, humidity, atmospheric pressure, and wind speed.

## Dataset

- **Source:** [Kaggle - Household Energy Consumption with Weather Data](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/orvile/household-energy-consumption-with-weather-data)
- **Total Rows:** 605,260
- **Total Columns:** 19
- **Preprocessing:** Data cleaning and transformation.

The dataset is not included in this repository due to its size.

## Key Analysis Areas

- **Energy Consumption:** Active power, reactive power, apparent power, voltage, and power factor.
- **Weather Patterns:** Temperature, humidity, atmospheric pressure, and wind speed.
- **Time-Based Trends:** Hourly, monthly, and quarterly analysis.
- **Weather Conditions:** Comparison of clear skies, clouds, rain, fog, and other conditions.
